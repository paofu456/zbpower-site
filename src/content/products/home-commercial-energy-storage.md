ZBPOWER supplies lithium battery systems for household and commercial energy storage. The range includes wall-mounted and stacked formats for different installation layouts.

## Storage applications

- Residential energy storage
- Commercial and industrial storage
- Grid and backup applications
- Solar self-consumption systems

## Installation formats

The range includes wall-mounted and stacked battery formats so the installation can be planned around the available space and system layout.

## What to confirm

- Required usable capacity and backup duration
- System voltage and inverter compatibility
- Wall-mounted or stacked installation format
- Available space, mounting and service access
- Target-market certification and installation requirements

Project requirements should be confirmed with the team before selecting capacity, enclosure or system configuration.
