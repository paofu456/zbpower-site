ZBPOWER's lead-acid replacement range uses LiFePO4 chemistry to support applications that traditionally rely on lead-acid batteries.

## Replacement applications

- Electric two- and three-wheelers
- Golf carts and low-speed vehicles
- UPS backup power
- Solar off-grid systems
- Ride-on toys and other compatible equipment

## Fit matters

A lithium replacement is not automatically interchangeable with every lead-acid battery. The existing installation should be checked before selection.

## What to confirm

- Physical dimensions and terminal position
- Nominal voltage and required capacity
- Charger profile and cut-off behaviour
- Continuous and peak load, plus BMS limits
- Enclosure, mounting and operating environment

Share the existing battery format, voltage and application with the team before selecting a replacement platform. The final configuration is confirmed against the equipment rather than a generic drop-in assumption.
