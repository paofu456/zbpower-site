ZBPOWER builds lithium battery packs for short-distance electric mobility, including e-bikes, e-motorcycles and cargo bikes. Product development can be aligned with the vehicle format, battery enclosure and target application.

## Support from selection to production

The team supports battery selection, prototype development and volume manufacturing. Share the intended vehicle, operating environment and enclosure requirements so the right platform can be evaluated.

## Applications

- E-bikes and cargo bikes
- E-motorcycles, mopeds and scooters
- Electric wheelchairs and mobility scooters
- Other light electric vehicle platforms

## Built around the vehicle

The battery is reviewed as part of the vehicle rather than as a generic catalogue part. The enclosure, mounting method, connector and removal process all need to suit the final product.

## What to confirm

- Nominal voltage, usable capacity and range target
- Enclosure dimensions, mounting and removal method
- BMS, connector and charger compatibility
- Duty cycle, operating environment and expected service life
- Target-market certification, labelling and packaging requirements

## From prototype to repeat production

Share the application, target volume and target market first. ZBPOWER can then review the battery platform, build samples for validation and prepare the approved design for controlled production and aging tests.
